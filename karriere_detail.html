Karriere

Köln/Bonn: IHK-geprüfter Schädlingsbekämpfer (m/w/d)

Leeser & Will Pest Control GmbH is one of the leading pest control companies in Germany. For over 75 years, we have been successfully combating pests of all kinds.

In doing so, we combine the great tradition of an owner-managed company with our high quality standards and the individual wishes of our customers.

We combat all types of pests throughout Germany for both businesses and private customers. Professional and effective pest control and customer satisfaction are our top priorities.

Our numerous satisfied customers rely entirely on our professional staff. Regular training makes them experts in their field.

Specializing in the care of large retail chains, Leeser & Will Schädlingsbekämpfung GmbH is in daily operation as a partner of well-known companies.

We owe our success to the work of our dedicated employees, whose passionate and professional commitment makes our excellent service unique.

To strengthen our team, we are looking for someone for the greater Cologne/Bonn area as soon as possible.

  • Entwicklung und Einführung neuer Bekämpfungsmöglichkeiten
